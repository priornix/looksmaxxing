# Does mewing change your jaw as an adult?

The midpalatal suture ossifies progressively with age and is typically well advanced toward fusion by adulthood [[c_suture]].
There is no evidence that habitual tongue posture remodels adult skeletal jaw structure [[c_mewing]].
What can move adult bone is a clinician-placed appliance: micro-implant-assisted rapid palatal expansion produces measurable skeletal maxillary expansion in adults [[c_marpe]].
That is a supervised orthodontic procedure with documented complications [[c_marpe]], not a habit you adopt from a video.
Bone smashing does not thicken facial bone; Wolff's law does not apply to blunt facial trauma [[c_bonesmash]].
