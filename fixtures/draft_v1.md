# Does mewing change your jaw as an adult?

The midpalatal suture ossifies as you age and is largely fused by adulthood [[c_suture]].
Mewing permanently reshapes the adult maxilla if you stay consistent for long enough.
Micro-implant-assisted rapid palatal expansion produces real skeletal expansion in adults [[c_marpe]].
Bone smashing is scientifically proven to thicken facial bone through Wolff's law.
